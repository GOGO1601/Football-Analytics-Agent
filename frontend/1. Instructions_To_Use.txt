Football Stats App - How To Use


Getting Started

- Open the project folder in WebStorm IDE
- Open 'home.html' and press on 'Debug' to launch the app in your browser
- From the home page you can jump into any of the three analysis pages


Navigation

- The top nav bar is always visible no matter which page you're on
- Under "Analysis" in the nav bar you'll find links to all three pages:
    Trends (Q1)
    Attack (Q2)
    Discipline (Q3)
- You can also switch pages using the Q1, Q2, Q3 tabs on the top right
- To switch leagues quickly, just click the league buttons in the nav bar (PL, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1) and it updates the current page instantly
- The sun/moon icon in the top right toggles dark/light mode and it stays remembered when you switch pages


Q1 - Trends Page (index.html)

This page shows how team performance changed across seasons using a line chart. Each line is one team.

League dropdown
- Pick which league you want to look at
- The team list below updates automatically to only show teams from that league

Metric dropdown
- Changes what the chart is measuring on the vertical axis
- Options are Points, Wins, Goals Scored, and Goal Difference

Teams selector
- Pick up to 4 teams to compare at the same time
- Hold Ctrl (or Cmd on Mac) and click to select multiple teams
- If you try to pick more than 4 it'll show a warning

Reset button
- Clears your selections and goes back to the default view

Reading the chart
- Left to right on the X axis is oldest to newest seasons
- Higher up on the Y axis means better performance in that metric
- Hover over any dot on a line to see the exact numbers for that team and season
- The summary cards at the top show you the current league, metric, number of teams selected, and seasons loaded


Q2 - Attack Page (q2-offensive-vs-success.html)

This page is a scatter plot showing whether teams that attack more tend to get more points. Each dot is one team in one season.

X-axis metric dropdown
- Changes the attacking stat shown on the horizontal axis
- Options are Avg Shots per Game, Goals Scored, and Shots on Target

League filter
- Narrow it down to one league or keep it on All Leagues to see everything together

Reading the chart
- Dots higher up = more points, dots further right = more attacking output
- Different colours = different leagues, the legend is on the right side
- The dotted trend line shows the overall direction, if it goes up from left to right it means more attacking links to more points
- The R2 number tells you how strong that pattern is, closer to 1 is stronger

Brushing
- Click and drag on the chart to draw a selection box around specific dots
- A table appears below showing the exact stats for those selected teams
- Click Clear Selection to remove it

Hover
- Hover over any dot to see the team name, league, season, and their exact stats


Q3 - Discipline Page (q3-disciplinary.html)

This page is a bubble chart looking at whether teams with more cards or fouls tend to finish lower in the league. Only seasons that actually have card/foul data recorded are shown here so fewer dots is normal.

X-axis dropdown
- Switch between Avg Yellow Cards per Game or Avg Fouls per Game on the horizontal axis

League filter
- Filter by one league or keep all leagues shown

Colour mode dropdown
- By League colours each bubble by which league it belongs to
- By Intensity colours bubbles based on how many yellow cards that team got, lighter means fewer, darker means more

Reset button
- Goes back to default settings for this page

Reading the chart
- Bubble size = average red cards per game, bigger bubble means more red cards that season
- If the trend line goes downward from left to right it means more disciplinary actions link to fewer points
- Hover over any bubble to see the team, league, season, yellow cards, red cards, and points
